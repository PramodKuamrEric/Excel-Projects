# 4. AOV Analysis

## Meaning

AOV (Average Order Value) measures the average value of an order.

```text
AOV = Total Sales / Total Orders
````

### Insight

AOV can be compared across cities, months, and customer segments to understand differences in average order value.

### Business Question

Which cities or customer segments generate higher-value orders?

### Further Analysis

Compare:

* AOV by City
* AOV by Month
* AOV by Category
* AOV for Repeat vs New Customers

---

# 5. ASP Analysis

## Meaning

ASP (Average Selling Price) measures the average selling price per unit.

```text
ASP = Total Sales / Total Quantity
```

### Business Question

Is the change in revenue being driven by more units sold or by higher average selling prices?

### Further Analysis

Compare ASP by:

* Product
* Category
* Month
* City

A change in ASP can be investigated alongside discount and product-mix data.

# 17. AOV and Profit Margin Analysis

AOV and Profit Margin provide two different business perspectives.

### AOV

Shows average order value.

### Profit Margin

Shows profitability relative to sales.

### Business Question

Which cities or customer groups have:

* Higher AOV?
* Higher Profit Margin?
* Both?
* Lower values for both?

### Further Analysis

Use a scatter chart:

* X-axis → AOV
* Y-axis → Profit Margin %
* Details → City or Category
